# Arcana

Tarot readings on a candlelit table. Choose a spread, shuffle the deck, draw your cards and turn them one by one. As each card turns, a narrator tells you its story, and at the end the whole spread is read as one tale.

**Open Arcana: <https://edmorais.github.io/Tarot/>**

Arcana works best on a phone, but it also runs on a tablet or a computer. Once it is installed, it works offline.

*Building or changing the app? See [Developers.md](Developers.md).*

## Install it on your phone

Open [edmorais.github.io/Tarot](https://edmorais.github.io/Tarot/) in your phone's browser, then add it to your home screen:

- **iPhone or iPad (Safari):** tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap **Install** when it is offered, or open the **⋮** menu and choose **Add to Home screen**.

Arcana then opens full screen, like any other app. It also works without a connection (see [Offline](#offline)).

## A reading, step by step

1. **Choose a spread.** On the home screen, pick a group (Core, Relationship & Decision, or Timing & Growth) and then a spread. A short note under the spreads tells you what each one is for.
2. **Choose your narrator.** The narrator is the voice that tells your story. You can change it later, even in the middle of a reading.
3. **Tap Begin the reading.** The deck is shuffled on the table.
4. **Draw your cards.** You can draw in one of two ways (change this in Settings):
   - **Pick from the fan:** the deck spreads out in front of you. Slide along it and tap the cards that call to you, or tap **Choose for me**.
   - **Cut & deal:** tap **Cut the deck**, and the cards are dealt into the spread for you.
5. **Turn the cards.** Tap the glowing card, or use the button at the bottom, to turn it over. Its part of the story appears as it turns.
6. **Read the whole story.** When the last card is turned, the narrator brings the cards together into a single reading.

Tap any card that is face up to see it up close, with its keywords and meaning.

The reading is saved to your journal by itself, so you never lose it.

## Moving around the table

- **Drag** to turn and tilt the table a little.
- **Pinch** (or use the mouse wheel) to zoom in and out.
- **Double-tap** an empty part of the table to set it straight again.

If you have moved the view, a small button also appears so that you can reset it.

### Card of the Day

When you draw your Card of the Day, it stays on the home screen until midnight. Tap it at any time to read it again. If you want a fresh one anyway, tap **Draw a new one anyway**.

## The narrators

- **The Oracle** (*mystic · poetic*): speaks in images and old cadences, like a fable read by candlelight.
- **The Guide** (*reflective · Jungian*): reads the cards as mirrors of your inner life, and leaves you with questions to think about.
- **The Friend** (*candid · practical*): tells it straight, with warmth, like a friend who happens to read tarot.

To hear the same cards in another voice, tap a different narrator at the top of the story panel. The whole story is retold at once.

## The decks

All four decks have the full 78 cards: the 22 Major Arcana and the 56 Minor Arcana.

- **Rider–Waite–Smith:** the best-known tarot deck, drawn by Pamela Colman Smith in 1909.
- **Tarot de Marseille:** the classic French pattern, from Jean Dodal's woodcut deck (Lyon, about 1701).
- **Celestial:** an original deck, a star atlas in gold foil. Its gold catches the light on the table.
- **Art Nouveau:** an original deck, a herbarium in the language of flowers, in inked pastel.

The narrators describe the picture on the card that is in front of you, so the same card reads a little differently in each deck. If you change the deck during a reading, the story is retold with the new pictures.

### Reversed cards

When **Reversed cards** is on, some cards land upside down. A reversed card usually means that its energy is blocked, delayed or turned inward, and the story takes this into account. If you prefer to read upright cards only, turn it off.

## Your journal

Tap the journal button at the top right to see your past readings. From the journal you can:

- open a reading and read its story again, or lay its cards back on the table;
- add your own notes, and mark readings you love with the heart;
- search by card, spread or note;
- see **Insights**: the cards that keep coming back to you, the balance of the four suits compared with chance, and the spreads you use most;
- share a reading as an image;
- delete readings (one at a time, or tap **Select** to delete several; you can undo straight away);
- export the whole journal to a file, and import it again, for example on a new phone.

## Settings

Tap the gear at the top right.

**The reading**
- **Narrator:** which voice tells the story.
- **Drawing the cards:** Pick from the fan, or Cut & deal.
- **Reversed cards:** allow cards to land upside down.

**Look & feel**
- **Deck:** one of the four decks.
- **Table:** Candlelit Velvet, Starry Night, Parchment or Slate & Stone. Each table has its own light: candles, starlight or a window.
- **Text size:** Small, Medium or Large, for the story text.
- **Position names:** show what each place in the spread means, on the table.
- **Graphics:** *Auto* gives full detail and switches to the battery saver if your phone runs slowly. *Battery saver* turns off shadows and floating dust. *Full* always keeps full detail.

**Sound & touch**
- **Sound:** soft shuffles, flips and chimes.
- **Ambience:** a quiet soundscape for each table, such as candle crackle, distant chimes, wind or dripping stone.
- **Vibration:** small taps of feedback, on phones that support it.

**App & offline**
- Install the app, get updates, and keep a deck offline.

## Offline

After your first visit, Arcana opens without a connection.

- The **Celestial** and **Art Nouveau** decks are drawn by the app itself, so they always work offline.
- The **Rider–Waite–Smith** and **Marseille** decks use scanned images. Cards are saved as you draw them. To save a whole deck at once, choose it and tap **Settings → App & offline → Keep this deck offline**.

When a new version of Arcana is ready, it waits until you close the app, so it never interrupts a reading. To update straight away, tap **Update** in Settings.

## Your privacy

Arcana has no account and no tracking. Your readings, notes and settings stay on your device, in your browser's storage. Nothing is sent anywhere.

This also means that if you clear your browser's data or uninstall the app, your journal is deleted. To keep a copy, export the journal from time to time.
